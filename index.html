Security-conscious by design

No extra company in the middle.

Many AI tools add another hosted service between your machine and the model. Whispkin is designed around user-controlled providers, including bring-your-own API keys and future local model workflows, so you can tune quality, latency, and token use.

Your chosen provider may still process audio and text under its own terms. Whispkin keeps that boundary clear instead of hiding it behind a bundled account.

## Short Description

Wispkin is a native macOS menu bar app that records your voice, transcribes it, cleans up the result with AI, and inserts the polished text wherever your cursor already is.

## Long Description

Wispkin helps developers use voice as a practical interface for LLM workflows. Instead of dictating perfectly, you can speak naturally: describe the change you want, outline a command, narrate a bug, or draft a prompt. Wispkin transcribes your audio, uses AI to correct recognition errors and wording, then delivers the cleaned command back to your active app.

Unlike hosted voice assistant products, Wispkin is built around user choice. You bring your own provider keys, choose the transcription and correction models that fit your workflow, and can use smaller models for low-token cleanup when cost or latency matters. You can avoid sending sensitive work through an extra intermediary service controlled by Wispkin.

## Value Props

- **Built for developer workflows:** Designed for prompts, code-adjacent commands, bug descriptions, refactor instructions, and fast LLM interaction.
- **Use your own AI provider:** Configure supported providers with your own API keys instead of relying on a bundled hosted account.
- **Control token use:** Choose lightweight models for fast, low-token cleanup or stronger models for more nuanced rewriting.
- **No extra company in the middle:** Wispkin does not need to proxy your prompts or audio through a Wispkin server.
- **Works where you already are:** Trigger recording from the menu bar or global hotkey, then deliver cleaned text to your current cursor.
- **Cleaner prompts from natural speech:** AI correction removes filler, fixes transcription mistakes, improves punctuation, and preserves intent.
- **Native macOS utility:** Lightweight menu bar experience with smart paste behavior and clipboard fallback.

## Feature Sections

### Speak Naturally

You do not need to dictate like a robot. Wispkin is designed to clean up the messy parts of spoken commands so your LLM receives a clearer prompt.

### Keep Your Provider Choice

Configure transcription and correction providers independently. Use the models that fit your cost, latency, privacy, token-use, and quality needs.

### Stay in Flow

Wispkin runs from the macOS menu bar and responds to a global hotkey, so you can capture a thought without switching apps.

### Reduce Security Risk

Many AI tools add another hosted layer between your machine and your model provider. Wispkin is designed so your audio and text go from your Mac to the provider you configure, not through Wispkin-operated infrastructure.
